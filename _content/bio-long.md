I am a PhD student in the Department of Biology at the University of Oxford. I work at the nexus of wetland ecology, nature markets, and nature-based solutions — asking how ecological evidence should shape the design of the financial instruments that increasingly govern landscapes.

My current research uses the National Wetland Condition Assessment (NWCA) to understand the drivers of wetland condition across the United States, and tests whether geospatial foundation models can predict wetland condition from remote sensing alone. Alongside this, I work on the permanence, additionality, and leakage properties of biodiversity and nature-based carbon offsets — most recently a review of permanence risks that produced an interactive database of the risks we identified.

I am committed to reproducible, open-source ecological data analysis, and write up the computational side of my projects as public walkthroughs.
